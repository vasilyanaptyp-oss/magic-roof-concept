## Inferences used in the concept

- The strongest commercial positioning is one direct contact for roof, waterproofing and matching building elements.
- The primary conversion should be a quote request, supported by a visible click-to-call action.
- Real project images should become the main trust signal once supplied by MAGIC ROOF.
- Until then, every stock image must be marked as a concept image and never presented as company work.

## Unknowns not invented

- Opening hours and emergency availability.
- Exact service area outside the Linz location.
- Project counts, years of team experience, certifications, partner brands and warranties.
- Prices, response times, customer ratings and testimonials.
- Official logo, brand colors and social profiles.
- Confirmed production hosting, analytics, form backend and consent-management setup.

## Competitor review

### e-dach.at

Useful patterns: clear service navigation, real project references, visible quote action and strong operational photography. Avoided: several competing actions above the fold and heavier overlay UI.

### ebner-dach.at

Useful patterns: prominent phone contact, separate Dachdeckerei and Spenglerei routes, references and contact kept easy to reach. Avoided: broad generic quality claims that MAGIC ROOF cannot yet document.

### rundums-dach.at

Useful patterns: short one-page journey from service promise to contact. Avoided: testimonial-led proof because MAGIC ROOF has no verified public review set.

### komplett-fenster.at and krug-systems.at

Useful patterns: product categories for windows, doors and sun protection. Avoided: long SEO copy and catalogue-first hierarchy that would weaken MAGIC ROOF's roof and waterproofing focus.

### kaanbauwerksabdichtung.at

Useful patterns: individual service explanations and direct contact details. Avoided: unverified service-area and availability claims.
